The Document panel is where a document's pages and its output settings live.  It shows the whole
pasteboard in miniature, one thumbnail per page, and lets you set the size, orientation and bleed
of the selected page, choose the resolution the document is prepared for, and add, remove and
rearrange pages without leaving the panel.  Everything you change here is part of the document:
collaborators see it as soon as it syncs, and it is remembered when you are offline.

== Opening the Document panel

To show the Document panel, do one of the following:

. Choose menu:Window[Document].
. Click the *Document* tab in the Properties panel group, if the group is visible.
. Click the *Document* button in the Main toolbar.  The button is not on the toolbar by default;
  add it as described in xref:customizing.adoc[Customizing toolbars].

The panel has four regions: the *Page Size* row (size pop-up, orientation buttons, *Edit…*), the
*Bleed* and *Printer Resolution* fields, the *Master Page* pop-up with its btn:[Edit] button
(xref:master-pages.adoc[Master pages]), and the *pasteboard view* with its magnification buttons.
The panel's Options menu (the small menu button at the top right of the panel) holds the page
commands.

== Setting page options

Page size, orientation and bleed belong to each page individually; a document can mix sizes.  The
options apply to the *selected* page, or to every selected page when several are selected with the
Page tool (xref:pages.adoc[Working with pages]).

To set the size, orientation and bleed of a page:

. Select the page: click its thumbnail in the pasteboard view, or click it on the canvas with the
  Page tool.
. Choose a size from the *Page Size* pop-up menu.
. Click the *portrait* (tall) or *landscape* (wide) button to the right of the pop-up.
. Type a bleed amount in the *Bleed* field and press kbd:[Return].

.Page Size pop-up
[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Item |Meaning

|Letter, Legal, Tabloid, A3, A4, A5, B4, B5
|Standard sizes.  Choosing one replaces the page's width and height; the orientation buttons decide
which dimension is the width.

|_Custom sizes_
|Sizes you have defined for this document (below).  They are listed after the standard sizes, in
the order you created them.

|Custom
|Keeps whatever width and height the page currently has.  A page shows as *Custom* after it has
been resized by dragging with the Page tool or after the custom size it used was deleted.

|Edit…
|Opens the *Page Sizes* sheet for defining and removing custom sizes.
|===

Bleed:: The distance objects may extend beyond the page edge and still be printed.  The bleed
area is drawn as a faint rectangle around the page.  Bleed is measured in the document's units; the
paper size chosen at print time must be larger than page plus bleed
(xref:printing.adoc[Printing]).

Orientation:: Changing orientation swaps the page's width and height around its top-left corner.
Objects on the page do not move or rotate.

NOTE: A page that is a child of a master page cannot be resized, re-oriented or given a different
bleed here; change the master page instead (xref:master-pages.adoc[Master pages]).

== Printer resolution

The *Printer Resolution* pop-up records the resolution the document is prepared for: 72, 144, 300,
600, 1200, 2400 or 3600 dpi, or a value you type.  It is used by flatness calculations, by raster
effects and by bitmap export as the default resolution, and by the output settings in
xref:printing.adoc[Printing].  It does not change how the document looks on screen.

To set the printer resolution, choose a value from the *Printer Resolution* pop-up menu, or type
one in the field and press kbd:[Return].  The setting is a document setting shared by every page.

== The pasteboard view

The lower part of the panel shows the pasteboard with a thumbnail for every page, numbered in page
order.  The selected page is highlighted; pages that other people are currently viewing carry a
small colored dot in their color (xref:presence.adoc[Presence]).

Magnification buttons:: The three buttons under the view show the pasteboard at small, medium and
large scale.  At the larger scales not every page fits in the view.  The magnification is a
personal setting: it is remembered per document on your Mac and is not shared.

To move a page on the pasteboard from the panel::
Drag its thumbnail to a new position.  The page's objects move with it.  The move is one undoable
change and is visible to collaborators when it syncs.

To scroll the pasteboard view::
Hold kbd:[Space] and drag inside the view.

To select a page::
Click its thumbnail.  Double-clicking a thumbnail also scrolls the document window to that page.

== Page commands in the Options menu

The panel's Options menu offers:

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Command |What it does

|Add Pages…
|Opens the *Add Pages* sheet: number of pages, size, orientation, bleed and, if the document has
master pages, *Make child of master page*.  New pages go after the selected page in page order and
are placed on the pasteboard to the right of the rightmost page.

|Duplicate
|Duplicates the selected page with all its objects, immediately after it in page order.

|Remove
|Removes the selected page and the objects on it.  If the page has objects, an alert asks you to

|New Master Page
|Creates a master page from the selected page's settings (xref:master-pages.adoc[Master pages]).

|Release Child Page
|Detaches the selected page from its master, copying the master's objects onto the page.

The details of each command are in xref:pages.adoc[Working with pages] and
xref:master-pages.adoc[Master pages].

== Document units

The unit of measure used by the rulers, the panels and every numeric field is chosen in the
*Units* pop-up at the bottom left of the document window.  The choice is part of the document, so
everyone editing it sees the same units.

To set the document's unit of measure:

. Click the *Units* pop-up at the bottom of the document window.
. Choose *Points*, *Picas*, *Inches*, *Decimal inches*, *Millimeters*, *Centimeters*, *Kyus*,
  *Pixels*, or one of the custom units defined for the document
  (xref:rulers.adoc[Rulers and units]).

Type sizes, leading and other typographic values stay in points regardless of the document unit.

=== Typing values in another unit

Any numeric field accepts a value followed by a unit abbreviation.  The value is converted to the
document's unit when you press kbd:[Return].

[cols="1,1"]
|===
|Suffix |Unit

|`pt` |points
|`p` |picas (`7p` is 7 picas; `7p6` is 7 picas 6 points)
|`i` or `in` |inches
|`m` or `mm` |millimeters
|`c` or `cm` |centimeters
|`q` |kyus
|`x` or `px` |pixels
|_name_ |a custom unit, by the name you gave it
|===

=== Arithmetic in fields

A numeric field also evaluates a simple expression: `+`, `-`, `*` and `/`, with multiplication and
division taken before addition and subtraction.  Units may be mixed inside the expression
(`2i + 3p` is 2 inches plus 3 picas).  A value without a suffix uses the document's unit.  If the
expression cannot be evaluated, the field shows the previous value and highlights briefly; nothing
is changed.

== Working with others

The Document panel reflects the document as everyone is editing it.  If a collaborator adds a page,
its thumbnail appears; if they resize the page you are looking at, the size pop-up and thumbnail
update under you.  Page settings you are in the middle of typing are never overwritten by someone
else's change: the field you are editing keeps your text until you press kbd:[Return], and then your
value is applied.

When two people change the *same* setting of the same page at about the same time, the change made
later stands.  {product} tells the person whose change was overridden ("Priya changed page 2 to A4
while you set it to Letter") in a brief notice, with a *Reapply mine* button.  Changes to different
settings -- one person changing a page's bleed while another changes its size -- both apply.

After you have been offline, your page settings are merged with everyone else's when you reconnect.
If the same page setting was changed on both sides, the review sheet lists it
(xref:collaboration.adoc[Working together]).

== Specification

=== Data model

The document setup chapter owns the `page` and `master` node kinds and the setup fields of the
`settings` node.  Pages are children of the well-known `pages` node (0:2); their sibling
`position` is the page order.  Masters are children of `masters` (0:3).  Everything is in points on
the pasteboard, y down, origin at the pasteboard's top left (xref:crdt-model.adoc[CRDT model]).

[source,protobuf]
----
// proto/wiretuner/doc/v1/document.proto (DOC epic; the file is normative)

enum Unit {
  UNIT_UNSPECIFIED = 0;      // reads as POINTS
  UNIT_POINTS = 1;
  UNIT_PICAS = 2;
  UNIT_INCHES = 3;
  UNIT_DECIMAL_INCHES = 4;   // inches shown as decimals rather than fractions
  UNIT_MILLIMETERS = 5;
  UNIT_CENTIMETERS = 6;
  UNIT_KYUS = 7;             // 0.25 mm
  UNIT_PIXELS = 8;           // 1 px = 1 pt at 72 dpi
  UNIT_CUSTOM = 9;           // see UnitChoice.custom
}

enum Orientation { ORIENTATION_UNSPECIFIED = 0; ORIENTATION_PORTRAIT = 1; ORIENTATION_LANDSCAPE = 2; }
enum GuideAxis { GUIDE_AXIS_UNSPECIFIED = 0; GUIDE_AXIS_HORIZONTAL = 1; GUIDE_AXIS_VERTICAL = 2; }

// The document's unit.  ATOMIC: a custom-unit choice is meaningless without the element id.
message UnitChoice {
  Unit unit = 1;
  ElementId custom = 2;      // element in SettingsProps.custom_units when unit == UNIT_CUSTOM
}

// Width, height, orientation and the preset they came from.  ATOMIC because a width from one
// user and a height from another is a page nobody asked for; the whole geometry is one choice.
message PageGeometry {
  string preset = 1 [(buf.validate.field).string.max_len = 64];   // "Letter", "A4", a custom size name, or "" for Custom
  double width = 2 [(buf.validate.field).double = {gt: 0, lte: 15984}];   // points, as oriented (222 in max)
  double height = 3 [(buf.validate.field).double = {gt: 0, lte: 15984}];
  Orientation orientation = 4;
}

// A ruler guide.  Element of a SEQUENCE on PageProps / MasterPageProps.
message Guide {
  ElementId id = 1;
  GuideAxis axis = 2;        // HORIZONTAL: a y position; VERTICAL: an x position
  double position = 3;       // points from the page's top-left corner along the axis
}

// Field blocks: 1 common, 2-9 DOC, 10-19 OBJ (names-notes), 20-29 FX (perspective).
message PageProps {
  CommonProps common = 1;                                     // name = the page's label
  Point origin = 2 [(wt.crdt.field).merge = MERGE_ATOMIC];     // page top-left on the pasteboard
  PageGeometry geometry = 3 [(wt.crdt.field).merge = MERGE_ATOMIC];
  double bleed = 4 [(buf.validate.field).double = {gte: 0, lte: 720}];   // points beyond every edge
  NodeRef master = 5 [(wt.crdt.field).on_dangling = REF_FALLBACK_UNSET];  // master node under 0:3, or unset
  Point ruler_origin = 6 [(wt.crdt.field).merge = MERGE_ATOMIC];  // zero point relative to the page's top-left; see the rulers page
  repeated Guide guides = 7 [(wt.crdt.field).merge = MERGE_SEQUENCE];
}

message MasterPageProps {
  CommonProps common = 1;    // name = the master's label
  PageGeometry geometry = 2 [(wt.crdt.field).merge = MERGE_ATOMIC];
  double bleed = 3 [(buf.validate.field).double = {gte: 0, lte: 720}];
  repeated Guide guides = 4 [(wt.crdt.field).merge = MERGE_SEQUENCE];
  // Contents: objects under layers with CommonProps.canvas = this node.
}

message GridSettings {
  double size = 1 [(buf.validate.field).double = {gt: 0, lte: 7200}, (buf.validate.field).ignore = IGNORE_IF_ZERO_VALUE];   // points between grid lines
  bool relative = 2;         // snap to the same offset within the destination cell rather than to intersections
}

// Size (width, height in points; ATOMIC wherever used) is in common.proto.

message CustomPageSize {
  ElementId id = 1;
  string name = 2 [(buf.validate.field).string.max_len = 64];
  Size size = 3 [(wt.crdt.field).merge = MERGE_ATOMIC];   // portrait width and height in points
}

// One custom unit equals `definition.amount` of `definition.base`.  ATOMIC so amount and base
// cannot be mixed from two writers.
message UnitDefinition { double amount = 1 [(buf.validate.field).double.gt = 0]; Unit base = 2; }
message CustomUnit {
  ElementId id = 1;
  string name = 2 [(buf.validate.field).string.max_len = 32];
  UnitDefinition definition = 3 [(wt.crdt.field).merge = MERGE_ATOMIC];
}

// The fields this chapter adds to the settings node (0:1).  Other epics add their own, each in
// the block recorded at the top of SettingsProps in document.proto.
message SettingsProps {
  CommonProps common = 1;
  UnitChoice units = 2 [(wt.crdt.field).merge = MERGE_ATOMIC];
  uint32 printer_resolution = 3 [(buf.validate.field).uint32 = {gte: 72, lte: 9600}, (buf.validate.field).ignore = IGNORE_IF_ZERO_VALUE];   // dpi
  GridSettings grid = 4;     // STRUCT by default: size and relative are independent choices
  repeated CustomPageSize custom_page_sizes = 5 [(wt.crdt.field).merge = MERGE_SEQUENCE];
  repeated CustomUnit custom_units = 6 [(wt.crdt.field).merge = MERGE_SEQUENCE];
  bool guides_locked = 7;    // View > Guides > Lock; shared, see the grid and guides page
  Defaults defaults = 10;    // OBJ block (default-attributes)
}
----

Not in the document: the Document panel's magnification level, the current page, zoom and scroll,
and the show/snap toggles of the View menu.  They live in the local store's `view` table
(xref:offline.adoc[Offline]) keyed by document id; they never sync.

Derived, never stored:

* *Page number* is the 1-based index of a page among the non-deleted children of `pages` in
  sibling order.
* *Page rectangle* is `origin` and `geometry`; the *bleed rectangle* is the page rectangle grown
  by `bleed` on every side.
* *Objects on a page* is a geometric query: a top-level object on the main canvas belongs to the
  page whose bleed rectangle contains the center of its bounds; an object whose center is on no
  page is on the pasteboard.  (Printing, which draws every object touching a page, uses its own
  intersection query.)

=== Merge semantics

[cols="1,1,3"]
|===
|Field |Policy |Concurrent behavior

|`PageProps.geometry`
|ATOMIC
|Two people setting the size of one page: the later OpId wins whole (width, height, orientation,
preset together).  Objects are not moved by either write.  The loser's write is retained in the log
and, because it is a same-register loss of the user's own visible work, always listed in the review
sheet.  Live, the loser sees the notice with *Reapply mine*.

|`PageProps.origin`
|ATOMIC
|Two people dragging the same page on the pasteboard: later wins; the objects on the page were
translated by each mover in the same change (see xref:pages.adoc[Working with pages]), so the
review sheet shows the objects' `move vs move` overlap as well.

|`PageProps.bleed`
|ATOMIC scalar
|Independent of geometry: one person's bleed and another's size both apply.

|`SettingsProps.units`
|ATOMIC
|Later wins.  Changing units never changes a stored value (everything is points), so a concurrent
edit typed in the other unit is still the number the typist saw.

|`SettingsProps.printer_resolution`
|ATOMIC scalar
|Later wins; listed in review when both sides wrote it.

|`custom_page_sizes`, `custom_units`
|SEQUENCE
|Adding on both sides keeps both, in order of insertion.  Rename vs. resize of the same custom size
both apply (STRUCT element fields).  Delete vs. edit: the element stays deleted; pages that used it
read as *Custom* with their own geometry, and the review sheet lists the edit-vs-delete with
*Restore*.

|`grid`
|STRUCT
|`size` and `relative` are independent registers.
|===

Read-time normalizations:

* A `PageGeometry.preset` naming a custom size that no longer exists (deleted, or from a template
  the document never had) reads as `""` (Custom); the width and height are the truth.
* A `units.custom` pointing at a deleted custom unit reads as `UNIT_POINTS`.
* `printer_resolution == 0` (never set) reads as 300.
* A document with no non-deleted page reads as having one Letter page at the pasteboard origin --
  it is not written until someone edits it (xref:pages.adoc[Working with pages] for how this can
  happen).

=== Offline behavior

Nothing beyond the standard outbox: page setting writes coalesce (a slider drag on the bleed field
is one op).  Presence dots in the pasteboard view disappear while offline.

=== Server

Nothing beyond storing and relaying changes.  The server validates the ranges above on every op.

=== Client

* `WTModel` exposes `Page`, `MasterPage` and `DocumentSettings` as typed views over the node tree,
  with commands `setPageGeometry`, `setBleed`, `setPrinterResolution`, `setUnits`,
  `addCustomPageSize`, `editCustomPageSize`, `removeCustomPageSize`.
* `WTModel.Units` parses field input: a decimal number, an optional suffix from the table above
  (longest match, case-insensitive), pica notation `NpM`, and infix `+ - * /` with the usual
  precedence and mixed units.  Formatting rounds to the unit's display precision (points and
  pixels 0.01, picas 0p0.01, inches 0.0001, decimal inches 0.001, mm 0.01, cm 0.001, kyus 0.1).
* The Document panel is a SwiftUI body hosted by the panel framework (APP-004).  The pasteboard
  view is an AppKit `NSView` drawing page rectangles and cached thumbnails from `WTRender` at the
  three magnifications; thumbnails re-render on `REND-004` invalidation with a 250 ms debounce.
* Numeric fields commit on kbd:[Return] or focus loss, never per keystroke; a remote value arriving
  while a field has focus is shown after commit.
* Undo grouping: each committed field is one change labelled "Change page size", "Change bleed",
  "Change units", and so on.

NOTE: As built by DOC-002 (`WTModel/PageSetup.swift`, `PageSetupCommands.swift`,
`PageObjects.swift`).  `PageList(state)` reads the live pages under 0:2 in page order (numbers,
effective geometry and bleed -- the master's while `master` names a live `master_page`, the
page's own otherwise -- own registers, ruler origin defaulting to `(0, height)`, guides) and the
live masters under 0:3; `DocumentSettings(state)` reads units, resolution, grid, custom sizes and
units, and the guide lock.  The normalizations above are read rules only: a preset naming no
standard or live custom size reads `""`; a stored geometry out of range reads Letter-sized; bleed
clamps to 0...720; a dangling or wrong-kind `master` reads unset; `units.custom` without a live
element reads points; resolution 0 reads 300; grid size 0 reads *12 pt* (one pica -- the spec
named no default, so this is recorded here); no live page reads as one Letter page at the
pasteboard origin with the id `PageList.synthesizedID` (0:2), and any command given that id writes
it first (`CreateNode`).  Commands, one change each: `SetPageGeometry` "Change page size",
`SetPageOrientation` "Change orientation", `SetBleed` "Change bleed" (children of a master are
skipped; a master page given as the target is written itself), `SetPrinterResolution` "Change
printer resolution", `SetUnits` "Change units", `AddCustomPageSize` "Add page size",
`EditCustomPageSize` "Change page size" (rename and resize are separate registers; pages and
masters using the size's name follow it in the same change, keeping their orientation),
`RemoveCustomPageSize` "Remove page size" (nothing else written: pages read Custom until a restore),
and for the rulers and grid pages `AddCustomUnit`/`EditCustomUnit`/`RemoveCustomUnit`, `SetGrid`
"Change grid", `SetGuidesLocked` and `SetRulerOrigin` "Move zero point" / "Reset zero point".
Objects on a page: `PageObjectIndex(scene:pages:)` bulk-loads a REND-003 `RTree` of the scene's
top-level objects' bound centres and answers `objects(on:)` (50,000 objects in well under the
5 ms budget), giving an object centred where bleed rectangles overlap to the lower-numbered page;
commands, which see only the state, use `PageObjects.objects(on:in:pages:)`.  Deviation: the
*Done when* asks for conformance vectors for the four normalizations, but they are read rules of
`WTModel`, not merge policy -- the `crdt-conformance` runners compare merged engine state, which is
the same whichever way it is read -- so they are covered by `PageSetupTests` and
`PageCommandTests` instead.  Also built on top: `WTModel.Units` (DOC-003, `Units.swift`):
`LengthUnit` (the eight built-in units and `.custom(id)`, their stored `UnitChoice`, factors and
display precisions) and `Units(settings)` with `parse` (a number without a suffix is in the
document unit, except as a factor of `*` or the divisor of `/`, where it is a plain number; a
length times a length, or division by zero, is invalid; a custom unit's name wins only when longer
than a built-in match; `p6` alone is six points), `format` (picas as `NpM`), `rounded` and
`convert`.  Not built: the Document panel, the Page Sizes sheet and the units pop-up (DOC-004,
DOC-005, DOC-014); the app's `DocumentHandle.pages` still holds the window's own page rectangles
and should read `PageList(model.state)` (with `frames(active:presence:)` for drawing and
`exportPages` for the Export sheet) when the panel lands.
