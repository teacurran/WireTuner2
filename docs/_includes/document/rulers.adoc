Page rulers run along the top and left edges of the document window and measure the pasteboard in
the document's units.  They follow the view as you scroll and zoom, show the pointer's position as
you move it, and are the place you drag guides from (xref:grid-guides.adoc[Grid and guides]).
The point they count from -- the *zero point* -- can be moved, and each page remembers its own.

== Showing and hiding the rulers

Choose menu:View[Page Rulers > Show].  A check mark shows when the rulers are visible.  The setting
is yours alone: showing rulers in your window does not show them in anyone else's.

Rulers use the document's unit (xref:document-panel.adoc[The Document panel]).  Large tick marks
are labelled in that unit; small ticks subdivide it, more finely as you zoom in.  While you drag an
object, thin lines on both rulers track its edges.

NOTE: Text blocks have their own text ruler for tabs and indents, shown in the block while you
edit text (xref:tabs-indents.adoc[Tabs and indents]).

== The zero point

The rulers count from the zero point.  By default it is at the bottom-left corner of the active
page, with x growing to the right and y growing upward, so that the Object panel's
position fields, the Info bar, and the rulers all agree.  Each page has its own zero point; when
you make a different page active, the rulers switch to that page's.

To move the zero point:

. Drag the zero-point marker -- the small crosshair where the two rulers meet at the top left of
  the window -- onto the pasteboard.
. Release where the new origin should be.  It snaps to the grid and to guides when snapping is on;
  hold kbd:[Shift] to snap to the active page's corners and center.

To reset the zero point of the active page, double-click the zero-point marker.

The zero point is part of the document, per page: everyone sees the same coordinates for the same
page, so a position read off the Info bar means the same thing to a collaborator.

== Coordinates in the Info bar

The Info toolbar (xref:toolbars.adoc[Toolbars]) shows the pointer's x and y relative to the active
page's zero point in the document's units, and while dragging, the distance moved and the angle.
Values there and in the Object panel are typed and displayed in the document's unit; suffixes and
expressions work in every field (xref:document-panel.adoc[The Document panel]).

== Custom units

Besides the built-in units you can define units of your own -- meters, feet, yards, miles,
nautical miles, kilometers, ciceros, didots, or anything else -- as a multiple of a built-in unit.
Custom units belong to the document and appear in the *Units* pop-up alongside the built-in ones;
to reuse them in new documents, put them in a template
(xref:templates.adoc[Templates and starting points]).

To define a custom unit:

. Choose menu:View[Page Rulers > Edit Units…].
. In the *Units* sheet, click btn:[+].  A new unit appears with a placeholder name.
. Type a name.  It is also the suffix you type after numbers in fields, so keep it short and
  unique (`ft`, `cic`).
. Type how much of a base unit one custom unit equals, and choose the base unit from the pop-up
  (for example, 1 `ft` = 12 inches).
. Click btn:[+] again for more units, or btn:[Done].

To delete a custom unit:

. Choose menu:View[Page Rulers > Edit Units…].
. Select the unit in the list and click btn:[-].
. Confirm the alert.  If the document was using the unit, it switches to points.

To edit a custom unit, change its name or definition in the sheet; every field using it updates.

== Working with others

Rulers themselves are yours -- showing them, and the way they follow your scrolling, affect only
your window.  The zero point and custom units are shared: if a collaborator moves page 2's zero
point, your rulers move with it the next time page 2 is active, and a unit they define appears in
your *Units* pop-up.

When two people move the same page's zero point at the same time, the later placement stands.
When two people define custom units at the same time, both units are kept.  If one person deletes
a custom unit that another is editing, the unit stays deleted and the review sheet offers to
restore it after offline work.

== Specification

=== Data model

`PageProps.ruler_origin` (ATOMIC `Point`, relative to the page's top-left in points, y down) and
`DocumentSettings.custom_units` are on xref:document-panel.adoc[The Document panel].  Master
pages have no `ruler_origin`; the master canvas uses the bottom-left corner of the master.

Default (`ruler_origin` unset) is `(0, height)` -- the bottom-left corner.  The rulers display
`(x - origin.x, origin.y - y)` in the document's unit, so y increases upward on screen while
the pasteboard stays y-down internally.

Why the zero point is shared document data and not local view state:: The zero point defines what
the numbers in the Object panel's X and Y fields mean.  Two people discussing "move it to
x = 100" must be reading the same frame, and a script or a print mark referring to page
coordinates must mean the same thing on every Mac.  It changes rarely, so LWW is
unproblematic.  Zoom and scroll, by contrast, describe
*where a person is looking*, not the document, and stay in the local `view` table.

Rulers visible:: Local `view` table, per document.

=== Merge semantics

`ruler_origin`:: ATOMIC, later wins; listed as a same-register loss in the review sheet when both
sides wrote it.  A reset writes the register to unset, so reset vs. move is the same case.

`custom_units`:: SEQUENCE.  Concurrent adds keep both.  Rename vs. redefine both apply (`name` and
`definition` are separate registers, `definition` itself ATOMIC).  Delete vs. edit: deleted wins,
review offers *Restore*.  Delete vs. choose-as-document-unit: `units.custom` dangles and reads as
points; review lists the edit-vs-delete.

Read-time normalizations:

* `ruler_origin` outside the pasteboard bounds (possible after a page resize) still applies as
  written; nothing to normalize.
* Two custom units with the same name: the suffix resolves to the one with the smaller element id;
  the Units sheet shows both and marks the duplicate.

=== Offline behavior

Standard.

=== Server

Nothing beyond storing and relaying changes.

=== Client

* `RulerView` (AppKit, `WTApp`): two `NSView`s in the scroll view's accessory positions
  (`NSScrollView.hasHorizontalRuler` is not used -- the ticks and the pointer tracker are custom
  drawn).  Tick spacing chosen from the unit's natural subdivisions (points: 1/6/12/72;
  picas: 1/6/12; inches: 1/2/4/8/16; mm: 1/5/10; pixels: 1/10/100) so that labelled ticks are at
  least 40 px apart.
* Zero-point drag is a temporary tool that draws crosshair guides during the drag and writes
  `ruler_origin` once on mouse-up (one change, "Move zero point").
* Info bar values come from `WTModel.Units` formatting the pointer's model coordinates transformed
  by the active page's origin.
* The Units sheet is SwiftUI with an editable table; each edit commits one change.
