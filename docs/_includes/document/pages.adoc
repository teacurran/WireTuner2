A {product} document has one or more pages laid out on a single large pasteboard.  Pages can be
any size, can differ from one another, and can sit anywhere on the pasteboard; objects can be
placed on a page, on the pasteboard between pages, or across a page edge.  You add, duplicate,
remove, resize and move pages with the Page tool on the canvas or with the Document panel
(xref:document-panel.adoc[The Document panel]).  Every page change is part of the document and
syncs to everyone.

== Adding pages

To add one page:

* Click the btn:[Add Page] button at the bottom of the document window.  The new page takes the
  selected page's size, orientation, bleed and master, and is placed to the right of the rightmost
  page, immediately after the selected page in page order.

To add several pages with specific settings:

. Open the Document panel's Options menu and choose *Add Pages…*.
. In the *Add Pages* sheet, type the number of pages.
. Choose a size (standard or custom), orientation and bleed.
. To make the new pages children of a master page, select *Make child of master page* and choose
  the master.
. Click btn:[Add].

To add a page by duplicating on the canvas:

. Select the Page tool in the Tools panel.
. Hold kbd:[Option] and drag a page to an empty area of the pasteboard.  The copy, with all the
  page's objects, is created where you release; it follows the original in page order.

== Selecting pages

The *active page* is the page that page settings, the ruler zero point and *Go to Page* refer to.
To select a page, do one of the following:

* With the Page tool, click a page on the pasteboard.  kbd:[Shift]-click adds pages to the
  selection; kbd:[Shift]-drag a marquee to select several.
* In the Document panel, click a page thumbnail.
* Use the *page selector* at the bottom of the document window: the btn:[<] and btn:[>] buttons
  go to the previous and next page, the pop-up lists every page by number, and the field takes a
  page number.  kbd:[Option]-click the buttons for the first and last page.
* Click a page with any tool, if the *Using tools sets the active page* preference is on.

=== Preferences that choose the active page

Both are in menu:{product}[Settings… > Document] (kbd:[Cmd+,]):

Changing view sets the active page::
When on, scrolling the document window so that a different page fills most of the view makes that
page active.  The page number at the bottom of the window and the highlight in the Document panel
follow.  On by default.

Using tools sets the active page::
When on, clicking on a page with any tool makes it the active page.  On by default; with it off,
only the Page tool, the Document panel and the page selector change the active page.

== Duplicating, removing and moving pages

To duplicate a page:

. Select the page.
. Do one of the following:
* Open the Document panel's Options menu and choose *Duplicate*.
* With the Page tool, kbd:[Option]-drag the page to an empty spot on the pasteboard.

To remove a page:

. Do one of the following:
* Select the page in the Document panel, then choose *Remove* from its Options menu.
* With the Page tool, select the page on the pasteboard and press kbd:[Delete].
. If the page has objects on it, confirm in the alert.  The objects are removed with the page.
  Objects that also extend onto another page or onto the pasteboard are kept.

A document must keep at least one page; *Remove* is disabled on the only page.  Removing a page is
undoable, and the page and its objects stay recoverable from the document history
(xref:history.adoc[History]).

To move a page and its contents:

* With the Page tool, drag the page to a new place on the pasteboard, or
* in the Document panel, drag its thumbnail.

To move a page without its contents:

. With the Page tool, start dragging the page.
. While dragging, hold kbd:[Cmd] and continue.  The page frame moves; the objects stay.

Pages snap to the grid while dragging if menu:View[Grid > Snap to Grid] is on, and to guides on
other pages if menu:View[Guides > Snap to Guides] is on.  Hold kbd:[Shift] to constrain the drag
to horizontal or vertical.

== Page order

Pages are numbered in *page order*, which is independent of where they sit on the pasteboard.  New
pages go after the selected page; duplicates follow their original.  To change the order, drag a
page in the *page list* -- the list view of the Document panel's pasteboard view, shown by clicking
the list button next to the magnification buttons -- or choose *Move Page…* from the panel's
Options menu and enter a new number.  Page order decides printing and export sequence and the
numbering everywhere in the interface.

== Modifying, resizing and rotating pages

To change a page's settings in a sheet:

. With the Page tool, kbd:[Option]-double-click the page.  The *Modify Page* sheet opens.
. Do any of the following:
* Choose a size from the *Page Size* pop-up, or choose *Custom* and type the width and height.
* Choose an orientation.
* Type a bleed.
* If the document has master pages, choose one from *Make child of master page*, or *None*.
. Click btn:[OK].

To resize a page by dragging:

. With the Page tool, click the page to show its eight handles.
. Drag a corner, top, bottom or side handle.  Hold kbd:[Shift] to keep the proportions.  Hold
  kbd:[Option] to resize around the page's center instead of the opposite edge.
. The page's preset becomes *Custom*.  Objects do not move.

Page edges snap to the grid while resizing when menu:View[Grid > Snap to Grid] is on.

To rotate a page:

. With the Page tool, click the page to show its handles.
. Move the pointer just outside the page near a handle until it becomes the rotate pointer.
. Drag.  The page snaps between portrait and landscape as you pass 45°; objects on the page do
  not rotate.

A child of a master page cannot be resized, re-oriented or rotated: edit the master page instead
(xref:master-pages.adoc[Master pages]).  Its handles are shown dimmed and the Modify Page sheet
shows the master's values disabled.

== Working with others

Pages are shared like everything else.  When a collaborator adds a page, it appears on your
pasteboard and in the Document panel; when they move a page, it slides to its new place, objects
included.  Page numbers renumber for everyone when a page is added, removed or reordered.  Each
collaborator's current page is marked in the Document panel with a dot in their color.

Two people editing the same page at once:

* *Both resize it* -- the later resize stands.  The other person is told and can reapply theirs.
* *One removes it while the other is drawing on it* -- the page disappears for both, but the
  objects the other person was drawing are not lost: they remain on the pasteboard exactly where
  the page was.  {product} points this out to the person who drew them ("Page 3 was removed by
  Tom; your 4 objects are on the pasteboard") with a *Restore page* button.
* *Both drag it* -- the later drag wins and the objects follow; the other person's drag is undone
  for both.
* *Both reorder pages* -- both reorders apply; if both moved the same page, the later one wins.

After working offline, all of this happens at once when you reconnect, and anything that collided
on the same page is listed in the review sheet, where you can keep yours, keep theirs, or restore a
removed page (xref:collaboration.adoc[Working together]).

== Specification

=== Data model

`PageProps` is on xref:document-panel.adoc[The Document panel].  This page adds no fields; it
defines how the commands write them:

Add:: `CreateNode` under `pages` (0:2) with `position` generated after the selected page's
position, `origin` right of the rightmost page's bleed rectangle plus one inch, `geometry`,
`bleed` and `master` copied from the selected page (or from the sheet).
Duplicate:: The add above, plus a `CreateNode` for every object on the page (objects-on-page
query, deep copy of each subtree, `origin` translated), in the same change.
Remove:: `SetDeleted(page, true)` plus `SetDeleted(object, true)` for every object whose bounds are
entirely inside the page's bleed rectangle, in one change labelled "Remove page N".
Move with contents:: `SetFields(page.origin)` plus a `SetFields(CommonProps.transform)` on every
top-level object on the page, one change.  Coalesces during the drag.
Move frame only:: `SetFields(page.origin)` alone.
Reorder:: `MoveNode(page, parent = pages, position)`.
Resize / orientation / rotate:: `SetFields(page.geometry)` (ATOMIC).
Modify Page sheet:: One change writing whichever of `geometry`, `bleed`, `master` changed.

=== Merge semantics

The nasty cases, by name:

Two people resize one page::
`geometry` is ATOMIC; the later OpId wins whole.  Objects are untouched by both.  The losing
write is listed in the review (same-register loss of visible work).  Live: notice with *Reapply
mine*.

Delete a page vs. add objects on it::
The page's `deleted` register becomes true; the concurrent object creations are not part of the
remover's change, so they apply and the objects live on the main canvas at their coordinates.
Objects are never children of pages, so nothing is orphaned; the objects-on-page query simply
answers "pasteboard".  The review sheet (and the live notice) lists "objects created on a page
that was removed" -- detected by `WTSync` as an overlap between a `SetDeleted(page)` change and
creations whose bounds fell in that page's rectangle -- with *Restore page*, which writes
`deleted = false` on the page.  Objects the remover deleted with the page and the other user
*edited* concurrently are ordinary edit-vs-delete entries with *Restore*.

Delete a page vs. move it::
The move applies to a deleted page; restoring the page shows it at the moved position.  No review
entry beyond the delete itself.

Two people move the same page::
`origin` later-wins, and each mover's change also transformed the page's objects.  Both object
transforms are ATOMIC registers on each object, so the later change's transforms win object by
object.  Because both changes are causally concurrent and translated the *same* objects, the
result is consistent (all objects follow the later drag) except for objects one user had added or
removed from the page meanwhile, which follow only the drag that included them.  The review sheet
lists the page as `move vs move`; *Use mine* re-translates page and objects together.

Concurrent reordering::
Fractional positions: both moves apply.  Moving the same page from two sides is a same-register
write on its position; later wins.  Adding pages "after page 2" from two sides gives two new pages
after page 2, ordered by their random suffix then node id; the interleaving is harmless.

Add pages while the other side removes the only other page::
Fine: the document always ends with at least one non-deleted page here.  The one way to reach zero
pages is both users removing different pages of a two-page document concurrently; each was legal
locally.  Read-time normalization: a document with no live page reads as one Letter page at the
origin, and the first command that touches pages writes it (`CreateNode`) with a review entry
"all pages were removed; a page was added" so both users learn what happened.

Duplicate vs. edit of the original::
Independent nodes; the duplicate holds the original's state as the duplicator saw it.

Presence:: The active page is presence, not document state.

=== Offline behavior

Standard.  A day's worth of page drags coalesces to one `origin` write per page.  Presence page
dots are absent offline.

=== Server

Nothing beyond storing and relaying changes.
