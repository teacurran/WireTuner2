Inspect mode is the document with its ruler out.  Instead of drawing, you point: hover an
object and its size appears; hover with something selected and the distances between the two
appear; click and a panel lists everything about the object -- its colors in whatever notation
you need, its stroke and fills, its type settings -- with a button beside each value that
copies it.  A developer building the screen you designed gets the numbers without asking; a
client reviewing the poster gets to look without the risk of nudging something; and a viewer
can copy an object out as SVG, CSS, Swift or PNG without an export dialog.  Nothing you do in
Inspect mode changes the document, so it is the mode {product} opens in for anyone whose role
is viewer.

== Entering and leaving Inspect mode

Choose menu:View[Inspect Mode] or press kbd:[Cmd+Shift+I]; the toolbar's btn:[Inspect]
button does the same and stays highlighted while you are in it.  The canvas takes a thin
border, the Tools panel collapses to the Pointer, Hand and Zoom tools, and the Inspect panel
opens.  Choose the command again to leave.

If your role in the document is viewer, the document opens in Inspect mode and you can leave
it to see the plain canvas; either way you cannot change anything.  Editors and commenters
open in the ordinary mode and enter Inspect mode when they want to measure.

Inspect mode is per window.  It is available in the read-only window of a version
(xref:history.adoc[Version history]) and in a branch (xref:branches.adoc[Branches]).

== Measuring by hovering

Move the pointer over the drawing:

* The object under the pointer gets a thin outline, and a label at its corner shows its width
  and height.
* With an object *selected*, hovering another object draws measurement lines between the two:
  the horizontal and vertical gaps between their edges, each with its distance.  Objects that
  overlap show the overlap instead.
* With nothing selected, hovering an object draws its distances to the four edges of the page
  it is on.  Hold kbd:[Option] to measure to the edges of the enclosing group or, for a
  top-level object, to the pasteboard's rulers' origin.
* Hover a point of a path (the Pointer's usual pick distance applies) to see its coordinates.

Measurements are in the unit and at the scale chosen in the panel's header
(<<units,Units and scale>>).  Hold kbd:[Shift] while hovering to keep the current measurement
on screen while you move the pointer to read it.

== The Inspect panel

Click an object to select it; the panel fills.  Every value has a copy button that appears when
you hover the row, and clicking the value itself copies it too.

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Section |Shows

|*Layout*
|Position (x, y of the top-left corner on the page), size, rotation, and opacity.  For a
selection of several objects, the bounding box of all of them.

|*Colors*
|Every color the object uses -- fills, strokes, gradient stops, text color, effect colors --
each with a swatch, its swatch name if it has one, and its value in the *notation* chosen in
the header: hex (`#E63946`), `rgb()`, Display P3 (`color(display-p3 …)`), OKLCH (`oklch(…)`)
or CMYK percentages.  Spot colors show their name and their process equivalent.

|*Stroke*
|Width, cap, join, miter limit, dash pattern, arrowheads, and the stroke's color.

|*Fills*
|Each fill in stack order: solid color; gradient with its type, angle and every stop; pattern
or texture with its name.

|*Effects*
|Each live effect with its settings (blur radius, shadow offset and color, and so on).

|*Typography*
|For text: font family, style name (Bold, Condensed Italic…), size, leading, tracking, baseline
shift, alignment, and the text style applied, if any.  Mixed runs list each run.

|*Text*
|The text itself, copyable as plain text.

|*Code*
|The selection as SVG, CSS or Swift, in a tab for each (<<code,Copying as code>>).

|*Export*
|btn:[PNG 1×], btn:[PNG 2×], btn:[PNG 3×]: copy the selection as a bitmap at that scale, or
kbd:[Option]-click to save it (<<png,Copying as PNG>>).
|===

The panel is a normal panel: dock it, tab it with the Object panel, or float it
(xref:panels.adoc[Panels]).  It is also available outside Inspect mode from
menu:Window[Inspect], where it reads the current selection.

[[units]]
== Units and scale

Two popups at the top of the panel decide how every measurement and every generated snippet
reads:

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Popup |Choices

|*Unit*
|*Document units* (whatever the document's Units popup says, xref:document-panel.adoc[The
Document panel]), *Points*, *Pixels*, *Millimeters*, *Centimeters*, *Inches*.

|*Scale*
|*1×*, *2×*, *3×*, or a custom factor.  Applies to pixel measurements and to PNG export: a
100-point-wide object at 2× reads `200 px` and copies as a 200-pixel-wide PNG.
|===

Both are remembered on your Mac and do not affect anyone else.  They are in Preferences under
*Sync and collaboration* as *Inspect unit* and *Inspect scale*.

[[code]]
== Copying as code

The *Code* section renders the selection as a snippet you can paste into a project.  Click
btn:[Copy] in the tab, or select part of the snippet and copy just that.

SVG::
The selection alone, as a standalone `<svg>` with a tight `viewBox`, using the same writer as
menu:File[Export] with its default options (xref:export-vector.adoc[Exporting vector
artwork]).  Names become ids; text stays text.

CSS::
Two blocks.  First, custom properties for every color the selection uses, named from the
swatch where there is one (`--brand-red: #E63946;`), then declarations for the selected
object: `width`, `height`, `background` (solid or `linear-gradient(...)`),
`border`, `border-radius` for rounded rectangles, `box-shadow` from shadow effects,
`opacity`, and for text `font-family`, `font-weight`, `font-style`, `font-size`,
`line-height`, `letter-spacing` and `color`.  Lengths use the chosen unit and scale; colors
use the chosen notation.

Swift::
SwiftUI.  A rectangle becomes `Rectangle()` or `RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius:)`, an ellipse
`Ellipse()` or `Circle()`, any other path a `Path { }` with its segments, each with
`.fill(...)` and `.stroke(..., lineWidth:)` modifiers; text becomes `Text("…")` with
`.font(.custom("Family-Style", size:))`, `.kerning`, `.lineSpacing` and `.foregroundStyle`.
Colors are `Color(red:green:blue:)` for sRGB and `Color(.displayP3, red:green:blue:)` when the
P3 notation is chosen; with a swatch name, a `static let` in a `Color` extension is emitted
first and used by name.

Snippets describe *appearance*, not layout: no absolute positions, no `frame` unless the
object has a fixed size.  What a snippet cannot express (a blend, a pattern fill, an extrude)
is represented by its flattened SVG and a comment saying so.

[[png]]
== Copying as PNG

btn:[PNG 1×], btn:[PNG 2×] and btn:[PNG 3×] rasterize the selection alone -- other objects
hidden, background transparent, effects included -- at that multiple of the document's points,
and put it on the clipboard.  kbd:[Option]-click a button to save the file instead; the file
is named after the object with the scale appended (`Logo mark@2x.png`).  The custom *Scale*
factor adds a fourth button.

== Links to an object

Any object can be pointed at with a link:

. Select it (in any mode) and choose menu:Edit[Copy Link to Object], or right-click it and
  choose *Copy Link*.
. Paste the link anywhere: a message, a ticket, a comment (xref:comments.adoc[Comments]).

Opening the link opens the document in {product}, goes to the object's page, scrolls it into
view and selects it.  Viewers land in Inspect mode with the object in the panel; editors land in
the ordinary mode.  Someone without access to the document sees the request-access page
(xref:sharing.adoc[Sharing]).  A link to an object that has since been deleted opens the
document and says so.

== Working with others

Inspect mode is a way of looking, and looking is visible: your pointer and your selection show
on collaborators' canvases as always, so pointing at an object in Inspect mode is a fine way to
say "this one" in a call.  Presence works as usual too: click an avatar to follow someone, and
your view goes where theirs goes while you keep inspecting.  To read *their* selection rather
than your own, choose menu:View[Collaborators > Inspect Selection] or click the name tag on
their selection outline; the panel shows what they have selected, marked with their name, and
follows their selection as it changes until you select something yourself
(xref:presence.adoc[Presence]).

Values in the panel are live.  If an editor changes the object you are inspecting, the panel
updates within a moment and the changed row flashes in their color; a snippet you copy is the
object as it is at that instant.

Nothing in Inspect mode needs the network: measuring, the panel and every copy work offline on
any document your Mac has.  Opening a link to a document you have never opened on this Mac
needs a connection.

ifdef::spec[]
== Specification

Inspect mode writes nothing and stores nothing in the document; it is a client presentation
mode plus a snippet library.  The server's only involvement is resolving a deep link's document
id through `DocumentService.Get`.

=== Data model

None.  Inspect mode reads `WTModel` and the merge state; snippets are generated from the same
display list the renderer uses.  The two popups are local preferences (`inspect.unit`,
`inspect.scale`, catalog category *Sync and collaboration*, `local`).  Deep links are URLs,
not data:

[listing]
....
wiretuner://doc/<document id>/node/<counter>-<replica>
wiretuner://doc/<document id>/thread/<counter>-<replica>     (comments, COLLAB-029)
wiretuner://doc/<document id>                                (document only)
....

`<counter>-<replica>` is the node's `OpId` in decimal; the app registers the `wiretuner`
scheme in its `Info.plist` (`CFBundleURLTypes`).  A `https://<host>/d/<document id>/n/<id>`
form served by the API is a redirect to the same `wiretuner://` URL for mail clients that
refuse custom schemes (COLLAB-038); the server does no lookup for it.

=== Merge semantics

None: Inspect mode never emits a change.  The panel observes applied changes to the inspected
nodes and re-derives its rows; the flash uses the attribution overlay of
xref:collaboration.adoc[Working together] (COLLAB-001) with the panel row as a second target.
Inspecting a collaborator's selection reads their `PresenceUpdate.selection`
(xref:presence.adoc[Presence]) and re-derives when it changes; a selection naming a node not yet
received shows *Waiting for object…* until it arrives.

Read-time normalization: none of its own; everything the panel shows is the renderer's
normalized view.

=== Offline behavior

Everything works offline on a cached store.  A deep link to a document with no local store
needs `DocumentService.Get` and `FetchSnapshot`; offline it shows "Available when online"
with the document's id.  A link to a deleted node opens the document and posts the status
notice "That object was deleted"; a link to a node not yet received (the client is behind)
waits for catch-up and then selects it.

=== Server

Nothing beyond `DocumentService.Get` for a deep link and the `https://<host>/d/…` redirect.
Viewer role: Inspect mode never sends a change, so there is nothing to enforce; the copy-out
of SVG, CSS, Swift and PNG is client-side rendering that a viewer is already permitted
(viewers may export and copy objects out, xref:sharing.adoc[Sharing]).  A workspace that
disallows package export does not restrict snippets; they are artwork the viewer can see.

=== Client

* `WTApp/Inspect`: `InspectModeController` (per-window flag; enters on open for `VIEWER`
  effective role; collapses the Tools panel to Pointer, Hand and Zoom; disables every
  document-mutating command through the command registry's validation so menus and shortcuts
  are inert), `InspectPanel` (SwiftUI sections above; unit and scale popups; copy buttons;
  Code tabs; PNG buttons), `RemoteSelectionInspection` (feeds a collaborator's selection into
  the panel until local selection changes), the menu:View[Inspect Mode] and
  menu:View[Collaborators > Inspect Selection] commands, menu:Edit[Copy Link to Object] and
  the context-menu item, and the `wiretuner://` URL handler (open document, wait for the
  node, go to page, scroll, select, enter Inspect mode for viewers).
* `WTRender`: `InspectOverlayLayer` above the presence overlay: hover outline, size label,
  gap measurements between the hovered and selected bounds (axis-aligned bounds of the
  transformed geometry; overlap shown as the intersection), page-edge distances, point
  coordinates, kbd:[Shift] freeze.  Uses `REND-003` hit testing with the Pointer's pick
  distances; redraws on pointer move and never invalidates document tiles.

NOTE: As built by COLLAB-035, the render half only (`WTRender/Inspect`).
`InspectMeasurements.measure(hovered:selected:container:point:origin:)` gives what the overlay
shows for one pointer position from axis-aligned pasteboard bounds the caller reads from the
geometry kernel (`Objects.bounds`, so the numbers are the kernel's): the hovered outline; with a
selection, the horizontal and vertical gaps (each through the middle of the span the two share,
or through the hovered object's middle) or, when they intersect, the overlap; without one, the
four distances to `container` (the page, or with kbd:[Option] the group or ruler area -- the
caller picks it); and a hovered path point read from `origin`.  `InspectFormat` reads points in
points, pixels (times the scale), millimeters, centimeters or inches with at most two decimals
("200 px", "120 × 48 pt"); *Document units* is the caller mapping the document's unit onto one
of these.  `InspectOverlayLayer` is a flipped `CALayer` of its own (so no document tile is ever
invalidated) that draws through `InspectOverlayRenderer`: outline, capsule labels, lines with end
ticks, the overlap tinted, at a constant on-screen size; `show(_:)` redraws only when what is

`InspectModeController` and the toolbar and menu commands (WTApp), and the 2 ms frame budget

* `WTInterchange.Snippets`: `SVGSnippet` (the IO-019 `SVGWriter` over a selection-only
  display list with a tight `viewBox` and default options), `CSSSnippet`, `SwiftSnippet`,
  `PNGSnippet` (the IO-021 rasterizer at `scale × device scale`, transparent background).
  Colors convert through ColorSync from the document's working spaces (D-041) to sRGB (hex,

  profile); a color already in CMYK reports its stored components.  Unit conversion is
  points → chosen unit, with pixels = points × scale.  Deterministic output: the same
  selection yields byte-identical snippets, which the tests snapshot.
* Undo: none.  Copy actions do not enter the undo stack.

NOTE: As built by COLLAB-035's app half (`WTApp/Collaboration/InspectMode.swift`).  menu:View[Inspect
