A document lives in one *space*: your personal space, or a team you belong to.  Who can open
it, and what they may do with it, is decided by the roles you give people and links in the
Share sheet, and -- for a team document -- by the team's default.  This page covers the Share
sheet, roles, links, teams and team libraries, and what happens to someone whose access
changes while they are working.  Accounts and teams themselves (signing in, creating a team,
company workspaces) are described briefly here and in full in
xref:security.adoc[Accounts, teams and security].

== Signing in

{product} asks you to sign in once per Mac.  The sign-in page offers a *passkey* first -- the
quickest and safest way: your Mac confirms it is you with Touch ID and there is nothing to
type or remember, and a passkey made on one Mac is ready on your others through iCloud
Keychain.  You can also *Sign in with Apple*, or use an email address and password; and if
your company has set up a workspace, choose *Sign in with your work account* and use the
company's own sign-in.  Whichever you pick, it is the same account: if you sign in with Apple
today and with a passkey tomorrow, using the same email address, you land in the same library.
If you hid your email address from Apple, add your work address on the account page before
joining a company workspace, so the workspace can recognise you.

The account page lists the Macs you are signed in on and how each one signed in, and lets you
add or remove sign-in methods; signing out of a Mac keeps the documents cached on it.

== Spaces: Personal and teams

You have one personal account and can belong to any number of teams at once.  The library
window's sidebar lists *Personal* and then each team; select one to see its documents.  A
document belongs to exactly one space:

* A *personal* document is owned by you.  Only people you invite, or who use a link you
  made, can open it.
* A *team* document is owned by the team.  Every member of the team may have access to it
  automatically, according to the team's default (<<team-access,Access for the whole team>>),
  and you can still invite individuals and make links.

To move a document from one space to another, choose menu:File[Move to…] in the library
window, or drag the document onto a space in the sidebar.  You must be the document's owner,
and moving into a team makes the team the owner; the team's default access then applies.
Moving a document out of a team needs a team admin.

NOTE: Creating a team, inviting people to it, team roles (owner, admin, member, guest),
leaving a team and binding a team to a company workspace are on
xref:security.adoc[Accounts, teams and security].  In short: members see and use team
documents; guests see only what is shared with them explicitly; admins manage people and
settings; and a team bound to a verified email domain can admit everyone who signs in with an
address on it.

== Roles

[cols="1,4"]
|===
|Role |May

|Viewer
|Open the document, see collaborators, follow them, browse history, export and print, and copy
objects out to another document.  Cannot change anything.

|Commenter
|Reserved.  There are no comments in this version; a commenter is treated exactly as a viewer,
and the role exists so that granting it now means something later.

|Editor
|Everything a viewer may, plus edit the document, name versions, create and merge branches,
restore versions, and invite other people at editor or below.

|Owner
|Everything an editor may, plus remove anyone, change anyone's role, change or revoke share
links made by anyone, set the team's access to this document, transfer ownership, move the
document to another space, and delete it.  A personal document has one owner: you.  A team
document is owned by the team; its owners and admins act as the document's owner, and the
person who created it is listed as the *creator*.
|===

Your effective role is the highest of: the role you were given by name, the team default (if
you are a team member and the document is in that team), and the role of any link you opened
the document with.  A role given by name can also *lower* what a team default would grant --
see <<team-access,Access for the whole team>>.

== The Share sheet

To open it, click btn:[Share] in the toolbar or choose menu:File[Share…].  It needs the
network: offline, the button explains that sharing will be available when you reconnect.

The sheet has three parts.

.People
A list of everyone with access by name, with their role in a popup beside each.  Owners can
change any role; editors can see the list but only change roles they granted themselves.
The person who opened the sheet is marked _you_.  Members of the team who have access only
through the team default are shown as one row, "Everyone in Marketing", rather than
individually.

.Invite
A field that accepts email addresses or the names of people in your teams, a role popup, and
an optional message.  Press kbd:[Return] or click btn:[Invite].  Each person gets an email
with a link; the document appears in their library under *Shared with me* (or in the team's
list) the moment they open the link, and in the People list right away.

.Links
Zero or more share links.  Each link has a role, an optional expiry, and optionally a
password.  Copy a link with btn:[Copy Link].

To invite someone:

. Open the Share sheet.
. Type their email address or start typing their name; pick from the suggestions if they are
  in one of your teams.
. Choose a role.  You cannot grant a role above your own.
. Optionally add a message, then click btn:[Invite].

To change or remove someone's access:

. Open the Share sheet.
. Use the role popup beside their name.  Choose *Remove* at the bottom of the popup to remove
  them.
. Removed people lose access at once (<<mid-session,When your access changes mid-session>>).

You can always remove yourself from a document you were invited to, from the same popup;
the document leaves your library and your local copy is deleted.

[[links]]
== Share links

A link grants its role to anyone who signs in and opens it.  Links are unguessable, and they
can be limited:

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Option |Effect

|*Role*
|Viewer, Commenter or Editor.  A link never grants Owner.

|*Expires*
|Never, or a date.  After the date the link stops working; people who already opened it keep
the access they have.  Owners can set *Revoke access from this link on expiry* to remove that
too.

|*Password*
|A phrase the person must type the first time they open the link.  Passwords are never sent
in the link and are not shown again after you set one.

|*Restrict to team members*
|For a team document: only signed-in members of the team can use the link.  Teams with a
company workspace can require this for every link.
|===

To make a link:

. Open the Share sheet and click btn:[New Link].
. Choose the role, and set an expiry and a password if you want them.
. Click btn:[Copy Link] and paste it wherever you like.

To revoke a link, click btn:[Revoke] in its row.  People who opened it lose the access it
granted (unless they also have a role by name or through the team).

There are no anonymous readers: everyone who opens a link signs in, so every collaborator in
the document has a name and a color, and history records who did what.

[[team-access]]
== Access for the whole team

A team has a *default document role* -- None, Viewer, Commenter or Editor -- that every
member (not guest) receives on every document in the team.  Admins set it in the team's
settings.  A document's owner can override the default for that one document in the Share
sheet with the *Everyone in <team>* popup, up or down.  A person's named role, if any, wins
over both when it is higher; when the named role is *lower* than the team's access, the named
role wins as well -- that is how you give a specific member view-only access to a document
the rest of the team edits.

== Requesting access

Someone who opens a link to a document they cannot see -- an expired link, a team document
they are a guest in, a personal document they were never invited to -- sees a page with the
document's name and a btn:[Request Access] button.  They may add a message.

Every owner of the document receives the request in the app (a badge on btn:[Share] and a
row at the top of the Share sheet's People list) and by email, and grants a role or declines
from there.  The requester is told either way.

== Transferring ownership

The owner of a personal document can hand it to another person:

. Open the Share sheet and choose menu:Actions[Transfer Ownership…].
. Pick the new owner from the people who already have access.
. Confirm.  You keep the document with the editor role; the new owner may remove you.

Team documents are owned by the team, so there is nothing to transfer; use menu:File[Move
to…] to move the document to a different space.

[[mid-session]]
== When your access changes mid-session

If your role is lowered, or your access is removed, while you have the document open:

* *Lowered to viewer or commenter*: the document becomes read-only at once.  A bar under the
  toolbar says "You can no longer edit this document".  Any changes of yours that had not yet
  been sent are offered as btn:[Save as a Copy…], which creates a document of your own with
  them, or btn:[Discard].  They are never sent to the shared document.
* *Removed*: the same bar with "You no longer have access", the same offer for your unsent
  changes, and the window closes when you have decided.  The document disappears from your
  library and your local copy is deleted after the offer.
* *Raised*: the document becomes editable without reopening it.

The same applies when you reconnect after being offline and learn that your role changed
while you were away: your offline changes are offered as a copy rather than being sent.

Your own presence -- cursor, selection -- continues to be visible to others as a viewer.

== Team libraries

A team can mark any of its documents as a *library*.  A library document's swatches, styles
and symbols (xref:swatches.adoc[Swatches], xref:styles.adoc[Styles],
xref:library.adoc[Library]) are then offered inside every other document in the team, under a
*Team libraries* section in those panels.

To publish a document as a team library:

. As a team admin, select the document in the library window and choose menu:File[Use as
  Team Library].
. Give the library a name (the document's name by default).

To use something from a team library:

. In the Swatches, Styles or Library panel, expand *Team libraries* and the library you want.
. Drag a swatch, style or symbol into your document, or double-click it.

What you take is *copied* into your document.  It becomes an ordinary swatch, style or symbol
of your document that anyone with edit access can change, and it remembers where it came
from: the Object panel and the panels show a small library badge, and hovering it names the
library.  If the library's version changes later, the item in your document does not change
by itself; choose menu:Actions[Update from Library] on the item to copy the current version
over it, or menu:Actions[Detach from Library] to forget the origin.  A library you have never
opened on this Mac is unavailable offline; ones you have used are cached.

NOTE: Team libraries are an addition.  {fh}'s Library panel held a single document's symbols;
{product} keeps that panel (xref:library.adoc[Library]) and adds shared libraries above it.

== Working with others

Sharing does not touch the document's contents, so there is nothing to merge: the People list
is what the server holds right now, and two owners editing it at the same time each see the
other's changes appear within a moment.  If two people change one person's role at once, the
later change stands.

After being offline, the Share sheet simply shows the current state.  Invitations you tried
to send offline were never queued -- the sheet does not open without a connection -- so there
is nothing to catch up.  Team library items you copied while offline (from a cached library)
are ordinary objects in your document and sync like any other change.

=== Data model

Everything here except library provenance is *server state*, not document state: the SRV-003
tables `document` (with `owner_account_id XOR team_id`), `document_member`, `share_link`,
plus `access_request` and `library` added by COLLAB-011 and COLLAB-012.  The shapes below
are the `docs.v1` request/response sketches that PROTO-007 and COLLAB-010 implement.

=== Merge semantics

Membership, links and requests are server rows with last-write-wins by server time; they are
not CRDT state and never conflict with document changes.  The document-side cases:

* *Copy from library* is a `CreateNode` (or a subtree of them for a symbol) under
  `swatches`, `styles` or `symbols` with `library` set.  Two people copying the same item
  concurrently produce two nodes; the panels show both, and either can be deleted.  There
  is no read-time deduplication (both are legitimate nodes).
* *Update from Library* writes the current library values into the existing node's registers
  and bumps `library.source_server_seq`; concurrent local edits of the same registers resolve
  by OpId like any edit.  *Detach* writes `library` unset.
* Objects that reference a copied swatch or style reference the *copy* (`NodeRef` to the
  local node), so the library going away, being unpublished, or the team being left changes
  nothing in documents that used it.

Role changes interact with the outbox, not the merge: on `RoleChanged` below editor or
`AccessRemoved`, `WTSync` freezes the outbox; unsent changes are moved to a new personal
document by `DocumentService.Fork` (snapshot at the last applied head plus the unsent
changes -- the same call as *Save my version as a copy*) or discarded.  The server would
reject them with `ROLE_INSUFFICIENT` anyway; the client never sends them.

Read-time normalization: a `library` whose `library_document_id` the caller cannot see is
shown as "from a library you can't access"; the badge still renders.

=== Offline behavior

The Share sheet, invitations, links, access requests, ownership transfer, publishing a
library and *Update from Library* need the network and are disabled with an explanation.
Copying from a team library works offline if the library's store is cached on this Mac (it
is cached the first time it is opened, read-only, like any document).  Role changes learned
on reconnect (`ROLE_INSUFFICIENT` on the first push, or `RoleChanged` in `Welcome`) take the
mid-session path above before any upload.

=== Server

SRV-010 delivers invitations, roles, links with expiry, and document events.  COLLAB-011
adds access requests, ownership transfer, link passwords, `team_members_only`,
`revoke_on_expiry`, the per-document team access override and space moves;
`SEC-001`/`SEC-002` (xref:security.adoc[Accounts, teams and security]) own teams, team
roles and workspace restrictions, which `ShareService` consults for `restrict_sharing`.
COLLAB-012 adds `LibraryService` and the read-access rule for libraries.  Effective role is
recomputed per RPC and pushed as `RoleChanged` to open sessions whenever a member row, link,
team membership or team default changes.

=== Client

* `WTApp/Sharing`: `ShareSheet` (SwiftUI sheet: People, Invite, Links, Actions menu),
  `AccessRequestPage` (the page shown by `OpenLink` failures), `MoveToSpaceSheet`.  The
  space switcher and team settings are `SEC-003`.
* `WTSync`: `AccessController` handling `RoleChanged`/`AccessRemoved`/`ROLE_INSUFFICIENT`:
  read-only flag on the document controller, outbox freeze, fork-or-discard sheet, store
  deletion on removal.
* `WTModel`: `CopyFromLibrary`, `UpdateFromLibrary`, `DetachFromLibrary` commands; a
  `LibraryCatalog` that opens cached library stores read-only and lists their swatches,
  styles and symbols for the panels.
* Undo: each library command is one change (`Add swatch "Brand red" from Marketing
  library`).  Sharing actions are not undoable (they are server calls); the sheet confirms
  destructive ones.
