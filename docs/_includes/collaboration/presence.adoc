Presence is everything {product} shows you about the other people in a document *right now*:
who is here, where they are looking, what they have selected, where they are typing.  None of
it is part of the document -- it is not saved, not in history, and it vanishes when a person
closes the window.  Its purpose is to make working together feel like sharing a table rather
than mailing files: you can see a colleague reaching for the object you were about to move,
and you can bring everyone to the page you are talking about.  xref:collaboration.adoc[Working
together] is the overview; this page has the details and the options.

== The avatar strip

Everyone with the document open is shown as an avatar at the right end of the document
window's toolbar, newest arrival last, with your own avatar at the far right.  Up to six
avatars are shown; more collapse into a "+N" button that lists the rest.  Each avatar has a
colored ring in the person's color.

Hover an avatar for a card with the person's name, their role in the document, and what they
are doing:

[cols="1,2"]
|===
|Card says |Meaning

|_Editing Logo mark_ |They are dragging, typing into, or otherwise changing that object.
|_Selected 3 objects_ |They have a selection but are not changing it.
|_Commenting on Logo mark_ |They are writing in a comment thread pinned to that object (xref:comments.adoc[Comments]).
|_Viewing page 2_ |They have nothing selected; the page named is where their view is.
|_Editing on branch "Autumn palette"_ |They are in a xref:branches.adoc[branch] of this document rather than in this document.
|_Idle_ |No input from them for two minutes.  The avatar is dimmed.
|_Reconnecting_ |Their connection dropped within the last few seconds.
|===

Click an avatar to follow that person (<<following,Following>>).  Click your own avatar for a
menu: *Spotlight Me*, *Stop Following*, and the display options below.

People who have opened the document only from a xref:branches.adoc[branch] appear with a
branch badge; people who are members but do not have the document open do not appear at all.
The Share sheet lists everyone with access (xref:sharing.adoc[Sharing]).

== Colors

Each person has one color in each document.  It is assigned the first time they open the
document, from a palette of twelve, in order, and it never changes for that person in that
document -- so "the orange one is Priya" stays true tomorrow.  When more than twelve people
have opened a document, colors repeat.  Your own color is shown on your avatar's ring and
nowhere else: your own selection and caret use the ordinary system highlight.

== Cursors

A collaborator's pointer is drawn on your canvas as an arrow in their color, with a name label
beside it.  The label hides after three seconds without movement and reappears when the
pointer moves.  While they are dragging with a tool, the tool's icon appears beside the name.
When their pointer leaves the canvas, their cursor disappears from yours.

Cursors are shown at your zoom level, in the position they occupy on the pasteboard, so a
cursor on a page you are not looking at is simply out of view.

== Selections and carets

An object in someone else's selection gets an outline in their color with a small name tag at
its top-left corner.  When several people have selected the same object, the outlines nest.
Their selection highlights points and segments too when they are editing a path
(xref:editing-paths.adoc[Editing paths]): the selected points show as small squares in their
color.

When someone is editing text, their caret is drawn in the text block in their color, with a
flag carrying their name; a selection range is shown as a tinted highlight.  Their caret stays
in place as you type before it, and yours stays in place as they type before yours
(xref:editing-text.adoc[Editing text]).

The Object panel adds a line under the object's name -- _Priya is editing this object_ -- when
the selected object is in a collaborator's selection and they are actively changing it.

None of these is a lock.  They tell you where people are so that you can avoid colliding, and
{product} merges cleanly if you do not (xref:collaboration.adoc[Working together]).

A collaborator's selection can also be *read*: click the name tag on their selection outline,
or choose menu:View[Collaborators > Inspect Selection], and the Inspect panel shows the
selected objects' size, colors and type settings, following their selection as it changes
until you select something yourself (xref:inspect.adoc[Inspect mode]).  Following someone
(<<following,Following>>) keeps working while you inspect.

[[following]]
== Following someone

To follow a collaborator:

. Click their avatar in the strip, or choose them from menu:View[Collaborators > Follow].
. Your view moves to match theirs: page, position and zoom.  A border in their color surrounds
  the canvas and a bar above it reads "Following Priya" with a btn:[Stop] button.
. Keep working if you like.  Selecting and editing objects does not stop following; your
  view simply stays with theirs.

Following ends when you:

* scroll, zoom or change page yourself;
* click btn:[Stop], press kbd:[Esc] with nothing else to cancel, or choose menu:View[Collaborators > Stop Following];
* follow someone else instead;
* or when the person you are following closes the document.

If the person you follow switches to a branch, following ends and the bar offers to open that
branch.

== Asking others to follow you

*Spotlight Me* asks everyone in the document to follow you -- for walking a team through a
layout, or getting people to the page under discussion.

To spotlight yourself:

. Choose menu:View[Collaborators > Spotlight Me], or click your avatar and choose it there.
. Everyone else in the document sees a banner: "Priya wants you to follow her" with
  btn:[Follow] and btn:[Dismiss].  Nobody is moved without clicking.
. While you are spotlighting, your avatar shows a small beacon and the menu item reads
  *Stop Spotlighting*.  It stops on its own when you close the document.

People who dismiss the banner are not asked again for the same spotlight.

== Display options

Each of these is in menu:View[Collaborators] and in Preferences under *Collaboration*; the
menu and the preference are the same switch.

[cols="2,1,3"]
|===
|Option |Default |Effect

|*Show collaborators' cursors*
|On
|Draws other people's pointers on your canvas.  Off hides them; avatars, selections and carets
are unaffected.

|*Show names on collaborators' cursors*
|On
|The name label beside each cursor.  Off leaves colored arrows only.

|*Show collaborators' selections*
|On
|Outlines, point highlights and text carets of other people.  Off hides them all; the "is
editing this object" line in the Object panel remains.
|===

All three affect only what *you* see; they never affect what others see of you, and there is
no way to be invisible to collaborators -- everyone in a document can see everyone else.

== Presence and the network

* While you are offline, others see your avatar marked _Reconnecting_ for a few seconds and
  then it disappears; your cursor and selection vanish with it.  When you reconnect you appear
  again with the same color.
* On your side, the moment the connection drops the other cursors freeze where they were.
  After a few seconds they are cleared and the avatar strip collapses to a single
  status: "Offline -- working alone until you reconnect".  Following ends.
* Presence is live only: no part of it is stored, so there is no presence in history, and
  nothing about who was looking at what is ever kept.

== Working with others

This page is about what you see of others; the same is true in reverse.  Your pointer,
selection and caret are visible to every collaborator in the document at all times, drawn in
your color with your name.  Your role does not change that: viewers are shown with cursors
just as editors are, so a viewer can point at things during a discussion.

After being offline, nothing about presence needs merging -- it simply resumes.  If people
joined or left while you were away, the strip shows who is here now.

== Specification

=== Data model

Presence never enters the document or the change log.  It is the `PresenceUpdate` /
`PresenceSnapshot` `UpdatePresence` calls up and `PresenceUpdate`/`PresenceSnapshot` frames of `SyncService.Subscribe` down (xref:sync-protocol.adoc[Sync protocol]),
extended here with the fields the guide needs.  The server fills `user`, `color_index`
and `branch` itself; a client's values for them are ignored.

[source,protobuf]
----
// proto/wiretuner/sync/v1/presence.proto (sketch)

// One collaborator's live state.  Sent by the client at most 20 times a second; the server
// coalesces and fans out.  Every field is "current state", never a delta, so a lost frame
// costs nothing.
message PresenceUpdate {
  // Server-filled.  The account this session belongs to, with display data so clients need
  // no lookup.  Ignored on the way up.
  Participant user = 1;
  // Server-filled.  Index into the 12-color palette, stable per (document, user).
  uint32 color_index = 2 [(buf.validate.field).uint32.lt = 12];
  // Server-filled.  Set when this session is on a branch of the document rather than on the
  // document itself (the branch's participants are relayed to the parent's session and
  // vice versa, so both sides see each other).
  string branch_id = 3;

  // ACTIVE, IDLE (no input for 2 min) or GONE (client sent a final frame on close).
  PresenceState state = 4;
  // Node id of the page the viewport is mostly on.  Unset on a master page canvas.
  wiretuner.doc.v1.OpId page = 5;
  // Pointer position in pasteboard points.  Unset when the pointer is off the canvas.
  wiretuner.doc.v1.Point cursor = 6;
  // Visible rect in pasteboard points and the zoom, for Follow.
  Viewport viewport = 7;
  // Identifier of the active tool ("pointer", "pen", ...), for the cursor badge.
  string tool = 8 [(buf.validate.field).string.max_len = 32];
  // Selected nodes.  Capped; a larger selection sends the first 200 and `selection_count`.
  repeated wiretuner.doc.v1.OpId selection = 9 [(buf.validate.field).repeated.max_items = 200];
  uint32 selection_count = 10;
  // Selected points or segments, as field paths into the selected path nodes.
  repeated wiretuner.doc.v1.FieldPath sub_selection = 11 [(buf.validate.field).repeated.max_items = 500];
  // Nodes the user is actively changing (mouse is down on them, or a field is being edited).
  // Drives "Priya is editing this object".
  repeated wiretuner.doc.v1.OpId editing = 12 [(buf.validate.field).repeated.max_items = 200];
  // Text caret, when editing text.
  TextCaret caret = 13;
  // True while the user is spotlighting.  Others show the banner once per rising edge.
  bool spotlight = 14;
  // The user this session is following, if any (informational: shown on the hover card).
  string following_user_id = 15;
}

enum PresenceState { PRESENCE_STATE_UNSPECIFIED = 0; PRESENCE_STATE_ACTIVE = 1;
                     PRESENCE_STATE_IDLE = 2; PRESENCE_STATE_GONE = 3; }

message Participant {
  string user_id = 1;
  string display_name = 2;
  // Avatar image as a blob hash, if the account has one; clients cache by hash.
  string avatar_sha256 = 3;
  wiretuner.account.v1.DocumentRole role = 4;
}

message Viewport {
  wiretuner.doc.v1.Rect visible = 1;   // pasteboard points
  double zoom = 2;                     // 1.0 = 100%
}

message TextCaret {
  wiretuner.doc.v1.OpId node = 1;          // the text block
  wiretuner.doc.v1.FieldPath text = 2;     // which RichText field
  wiretuner.doc.v1.ElementId position = 3; // character id the caret is before; zero = end
  // Selection range end when a range is selected; unset for a bare caret.
  wiretuner.doc.v1.ElementId range_end = 4;
}

// Sent once on join and whenever the server rebuilds it: everyone currently present.
message PresenceSnapshot { repeated PresenceUpdate participants = 1; }

// Added to SequencedChange so attribution needs no replica lookup (used by
// xref:collaboration.adoc[Working together] and xref:history.adoc[Version history]).
message SequencedChange {
  uint64 server_seq = 1;
  wiretuner.doc.v1.Change change = 2;
  Participant author = 3;   // server-filled from the replica binding
}
----

Color assignment is server state: `document_member.color_index` (SRV-003 schema, extended by
COLLAB-005), assigned on the first `Subscribe` from that user as the lowest palette index not in
use among members who have ever opened the document, wrapping after twelve.  Users with
access through a team default or a link, who have no `document_member` row, get one created
with `role = NONE` purely to hold the color.

=== Merge semantics

None.  Presence is not merged; the newest frame per session replaces the previous one, and a
session's entry expires 15 s after its last frame.  Two sessions from one user (two Macs)
are two participants with the same color.

The only document-adjacent behavior is caret placement: a `TextCaret` names a character id,
so it survives concurrent edits by construction (the id does not move); the layout engine
maps it to a glyph position (TXT-001).  A caret whose character was deleted is drawn before
the next surviving character.

Read-time normalization: a presence entry naming a node that does not exist locally yet (the
change creating it has not arrived) is kept and shown as soon as the node appears; one naming
a deleted node is not drawn.

=== Offline behavior

Presence requires the session.  On disconnect the client marks every remote participant
frozen; after 5 s it clears them and shows the offline status.  On reconnect, `Subscribe` delivers
a `PresenceSnapshot` after replay and normal operation resumes.  Outgoing presence while
offline is dropped, not queued.

=== Server

SRV-012 relays presence.  COLLAB-005 adds: color assignment on first join, validation and
truncation of the caps above, filling of the server-owned fields, relaying between a branch
session and its parent's sessions, and `author` on every `SequencedChange`.  Spotlight is an
ordinary presence field; the server does nothing special with it.

=== Client

* `WTSync.PresenceModel` (SYNC-009): an observable dictionary `session id → PresenceUpdate`
  with the local user excluded, published on the main actor.
* `WTRender`: a `PresenceOverlayLayer` above the tiled canvas draws cursors, labels,
  selection outlines, point highlights, carets and attribution flashes.  It is redrawn on
  presence changes and on scroll/zoom, never invalidating document tiles.  Hit testing
  ignores it.
* `WTApp/Collaboration`: `AvatarStripView` (SwiftUI in the toolbar), `FollowController`
  (drives the canvas scroll/zoom from a participant's `viewport`, ends on local navigation),
  `SpotlightBanner`, the menu:View[Collaborators] menu bound to the three preferences.
* Outgoing presence: `WTApp` publishes cursor, viewport, selection, editing set and caret to
  `WTSync`, which coalesces at 20 Hz.  `editing` is set for the duration of a mouse-down drag
  on a node and while an Object panel field on it has focus, and carries a `comment_thread`
  node id while a thread's composer has focus (xref:comments.adoc[Comments]); the hover card
  renders that as _Commenting on <anchor name>_.
* Inspecting a collaborator's selection (xref:inspect.adoc[Inspect mode], COLLAB-037) reads
  `selection` from `PresenceModel`; nothing is sent for it.
* Idle: two minutes without key or mouse input sets `IDLE`; any input clears it.
* No undo involvement: nothing here writes to the document.
