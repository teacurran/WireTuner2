Every change anyone makes to a document is kept, with who made it and when.  Version history
is where you look at that record: to see what happened while you were away, to find out who
moved the logo, to name the state you are about to send to the printer, and to get back to
how things were on Tuesday -- without stopping anyone else from working.  There is no Save;
history is written as you draw (xref:saving.adoc[Saving]).

== The History panel

Open it with menu:Window[History] or menu:File[Show History].  The panel is a timeline of the
document, newest at the top:

* *Named versions* stand out as bold rows with their name, who named them and when.
* Between them, *changes* are grouped into sessions: one row per person per stretch of work,
  such as "Priya · 14:02–15:40 · 312 changes".  Expand a session to see its individual
  changes by label -- "Move 3 objects", "Change fill of Logo mark", "Type in Tagline".
* Changes that arrived from a merged xref:branches.adoc[branch] are grouped under the branch's
  name with the person who merged it.
* Your own changes made offline and not yet sent are at the top under *Not yet synced*, in
  italics.
* Comments (xref:comments.adoc[Comments]) are changes too, and appear by label -- "Comment on
  Logo mark", "Resolve thread 14" -- inside their author's session.  A *Hide comments* switch
  in the panel's toolbar keeps them out of the timeline.

Hover a row for its actions: btn:[View], btn:[Restore], btn:[Compare].  Right-click a named
version for *Rename* and *Delete*.

The panel searches: type a name, a label or an object name in its search field to filter the
timeline to matching rows.

== Naming a version

A named version is a bookmark on the timeline: "Sent to printer", "Client review 2".  Naming
does not copy anything or stop anything; it makes a moment easy to find and keeps it beyond
the ordinary retention (<<retention,How long history is kept>>).

To name the current state:

. Press kbd:[Cmd+S], or choose menu:File[Name This Version…].
. Type a name and, optionally, a note.  Click btn:[Name].
. The version appears at the top of the History panel.  Everyone with the document open sees
  it.

NOTE: kbd:[Cmd+S] does not save.  The document is always saved; kbd:[Cmd+S] names a version.

To rename or delete a named version, right-click it in the History panel.  Deleting the name
does not delete the changes; it only removes the bookmark (and, once the changes are older
than the retention window, the ability to view that exact state).

== Viewing a version

To look at the document as it was at any row in the timeline:

. Hover the row and click btn:[View], or double-click it.
. A read-only window opens, titled "Catalogue — 'Client review 2' (read-only)", with a bar
  above the canvas offering btn:[Restore], btn:[Restore as Copy…] and btn:[Compare with
  Current].
. Browse, select, zoom, copy objects out.  Nothing you do here changes anything.  The window
  supports xref:inspect.adoc[Inspect mode], so you can measure the old state and copy its
  colors and code just as you can in the live document.

Viewing a version is private: collaborators do not see you looking, and the live document
is unaffected.

== Restoring a version

There are two ways back.  Both leave everyone else's work intact.

Restore as the current state::
Makes the live document look like the version again.  {product} works out what differs
between the version and the document as it is now, and applies those differences *as a new
change* by you, labelled "Restore 'Client review 2'".  Nothing is rewound: every change made
since is still in history, the restore itself is in history, and anyone working in the
document at that moment sees the objects change with your name on them, just as for any
edit.  Objects they are editing right then merge with the restore attribute by attribute --
if Tom is dragging the logo while you restore, the logo ends up wherever the later of the two
changes put it.  Restore is undoable like any change.  Comments are left alone: restoring
does not remove threads written since the version or bring back deleted comments.

Restore as a copy::
Creates a *new document* in your space holding the version's state, and leaves the live
document alone.  Use it when you want the old state next to the new one, or when a client
wants "the version from last week" as its own file.

To restore:

. In the History panel, hover the row and click btn:[Restore]; or in a version's read-only
  window, click btn:[Restore] or btn:[Restore as Copy…] in the bar.
. For a restore to current, a summary appears: "This will change 41 objects, delete 3
  objects added since, and bring back 2 deleted objects."  Click btn:[Restore].
. For a copy, name the new document.  It opens in a new window.

You need the editor role to restore to current; anyone can restore as a copy.

== Comparing two versions

To see what changed between any two rows:

. Select a row in the History panel, then kbd:[Cmd]-click a second row.
. Click btn:[Compare] in the panel's toolbar (or choose *Compare with Current* on a single
  row).
. The Review Changes sheet opens in compare mode (xref:collaboration.adoc#review[The Review
  Changes sheet]): every object that differs between the two, with btn:[Older], btn:[Newer]
  and btn:[Overlay] previews and the attribute-by-attribute list.  It is read-only; close it
  when done.

== Who changed this object

Select any object and look at the top of the Object panel (xref:object-panel.adoc[Object
panel]): under its name is a line such as _Changed by Priya, 5 minutes ago_.  Click it for a
popover listing the object's own history -- each change to it, by whom, when, and what was
changed ("Fill", "Position", "Text") -- with btn:[View] on each row to open the document as it
was just before that change.  This is the quickest answer to "who moved this and when".

== Undo, history and other people

Undo (xref:undo.adoc[Undoing actions]) is yours alone: it steps back through *your* changes
in this window and never touches anyone else's.  History is everyone's: it holds every
change from every person, in the order they arrived.  If you need to take back something a
collaborator did, do it from history -- view the state before their change and restore, or
use the object's history popover -- rather than expecting undo to reach it.

[[retention]]
== How long history is kept

* Every change is kept for *30 days*.  Within that window you can view, compare and restore
  any moment and see every row in the timeline.
* *Named versions are kept until you delete them.*  A named version older than 30 days can
  still be viewed, compared, and restored, but the individual changes leading up to it are
  summarized ("Priya · 14 March · 312 changes") rather than listed.
* Deleted objects can be brought back for 30 days from the History panel; after that they
  survive only inside named versions.
* Teams can lengthen the 30-day window in team settings (xref:security.adoc[Accounts, teams
  and security]).

== Working with others

The History panel is live: a collaborator's changes appear at the top as they happen, and a
version they name appears for you at once.  Two people naming a version in the same second
get two named versions, one each.

When you restore a version while others are working, they see it as a change by you -- the
affected objects pulse with your name -- and their own in-progress edits merge with it
attribute by attribute.  Nobody is interrupted and nobody's work disappears; if the restore
overrode something they had just done, it is in history and they can restore it in turn.

After being offline, the timeline shows your changes under *Not yet synced* until they are
sent; versions you named offline are marked the same way and become ordinary named versions
when you reconnect.  You can view and restore versions your Mac already knows about while
offline; older history downloads when you are back online.

== Specification

=== Data model

The history *is* the change log (xref:crdt-model.adoc[CRDT model],
xref:sync-protocol.adoc[Sync protocol]): hot rows in Postgres, cold segments in object
storage, with `Change.label`, `wall_time_ms` and the server-attached author.  Named versions
are server rows (`version`: document, `server_seq`, name, note, creator, timestamps, plus a
materialized snapshot reference).  Nothing new enters the document schema; a *restore* is an
ordinary `Change`.

[source,protobuf]
----
// proto/wiretuner/docs/v1/version.proto (sketch)
service VersionService {
  // The timeline: change summaries newest first, already grouped into sessions by the server
  // (same author, gaps under 10 min), interleaved with named versions.
  rpc ListHistory(ListHistoryRequest) returns (ListHistoryResponse);
  // Every change that touched one node (blame), newest first.
  rpc ListNodeHistory(ListNodeHistoryRequest) returns (ListNodeHistoryResponse);
  rpc ListVersions(ListVersionsRequest) returns (ListVersionsResponse);
  rpc NameVersion(NameVersionRequest) returns (NameVersionResponse);
  rpc UpdateVersion(UpdateVersionRequest) returns (UpdateVersionResponse);   // rename, note
  rpc DeleteVersion(DeleteVersionRequest) returns (DeleteVersionResponse);
  // A new document from the state at a server_seq (DocumentService.Fork with no changes).
  rpc RestoreAsCopy(RestoreAsCopyRequest) returns (RestoreAsCopyResponse);
}

message ListHistoryRequest {
  string document_id = 1;
  // Page backwards from here (0 = head).  Opaque cursor in the response continues.
  uint64 before_server_seq = 2;
  string cursor = 3;
  uint32 limit = 4 [(buf.validate.field).uint32.lte = 500];
  string query = 5;   // label / author / object-name filter, server-side
}
message ListHistoryResponse {
  repeated HistoryRow rows = 1;
  string next_cursor = 2;
  // Below this seq only named versions are viewable (retention window).
  uint64 retained_from_seq = 3;
}
message HistoryRow {
  oneof row {
    Session session = 1;
    Version version = 2;
  }
}
// A stretch of one person's work.  Individual changes are fetched by expanding the session.
message Session {
  wiretuner.sync.v1.Participant author = 1;
  uint64 first_server_seq = 2;
  uint64 last_server_seq = 3;
  google.protobuf.Timestamp started_at = 4;
  google.protobuf.Timestamp ended_at = 5;
  uint32 change_count = 6;
  uint32 node_count = 7;                 // distinct nodes touched
  string merged_from_branch_id = 8;      // set when the session is a branch merge
  string merged_from_branch_name = 9;
  // Present when the session is small or expanded: labels for the rows.
  repeated ChangeSummary changes = 10;
}
message ChangeSummary {
  uint64 server_seq = 1;
  string label = 2;
  google.protobuf.Timestamp wall_time = 3;
  uint32 op_count = 4;
  // Up to 8 touched nodes with their Object-panel names at the time, for the row text.
  repeated NamedNode nodes = 5;
  // Register names written, for blame ("Fill", "Position"): derived from field paths through
  // the merge table's display names.
  repeated string attributes = 6;
}
message NamedNode { wiretuner.doc.v1.OpId id = 1; string name = 2; }

message ListNodeHistoryRequest {
  string document_id = 1;
  wiretuner.doc.v1.OpId node = 2;
  string cursor = 3;
  uint32 limit = 4 [(buf.validate.field).uint32.lte = 200];
}
message ListNodeHistoryResponse {
  repeated ChangeSummary changes = 1;    // each with `author` via the session it belongs to
  repeated wiretuner.sync.v1.Participant authors = 2;   // indexed by ChangeSummary order
  string next_cursor = 3;
}

message Version {
  string id = 1;
  string document_id = 2;
  uint64 server_seq = 3;                 // the state "at" this seq, inclusive
  string name = 4 [(buf.validate.field).string.max_len = 256];
  string note = 5 [(buf.validate.field).string.max_len = 8192];
  wiretuner.sync.v1.Participant created_by = 6;
  google.protobuf.Timestamp created_at = 7;
  // True once the server has materialized a snapshot at exactly `server_seq`, which makes
  // the version viewable after its changes leave the retention window.
  bool pinned = 8;
}
message ListVersionsRequest { string document_id = 1; string cursor = 2; }
message ListVersionsResponse { repeated Version versions = 1; string next_cursor = 2; }
message NameVersionRequest {
  string document_id = 1;
  string version_id = 2;                 // client UUIDv7 (idempotent; also the offline key)
  // The head the client had applied when the user named it.  For a version named offline
  // this is the last remote head plus the client's own changes: the server records the seq
  // at which the last of those changes was sequenced, so the version means "my state then".
  uint64 server_seq = 3;
  wiretuner.doc.v1.OpId through_local_change = 4;   // last local change included (offline case)
  string name = 5;
  string note = 6;
}
message NameVersionResponse { Version version = 1; }
message UpdateVersionRequest { string version_id = 1; optional string name = 2; optional string note = 3; }
message UpdateVersionResponse { Version version = 1; }
message DeleteVersionRequest { string version_id = 1; }
message DeleteVersionResponse {}
message RestoreAsCopyRequest {
  string document_id = 1;
  uint64 server_seq = 2;
  string new_document_id = 3;            // client UUIDv7
  string name = 4;
}
message RestoreAsCopyResponse { string new_document_id = 1; }
----

As built by PROTO-007 (`proto/wiretuner/docs/v1/version.proto`): `limit` is `page_size`, as
in every other list of the API; `ListHistoryRequest.expand_session` asks for one session's
individual changes; `PinVersion(version_id, pinned)` materializes (or releases) the snapshot
that `Version.pinned` reports; `RestoreAsCopy` takes `space_id`/`folder_id` and returns the
new `Document`; `Version.id` and `NameVersionRequest.version_id` are client UUIDv7s.  Roles:
any role reads, editor or owner names, pins, updates and deletes.

=== Merge semantics

Restore to current::
The client builds the state at the version's seq (`FetchSnapshot` at or before it, then
`FetchChanges` to exactly it, replayed in `WTCRDT` into a scratch document; or from the local
log when the seq is within it) and diffs it against the current state.  The diff becomes one
`Change` labelled `Restore '<name>'`, containing, per node:
+
* present in the version, `deleted` now → `SetDeleted(false)`;
* created after the version (absent from it) → `SetDeleted(true)`;
* any register whose value differs → `SetFields` with the version's value (one op per node,
  all paths);
* parent or position differ → `MoveNode` to the version's parent and position;
* sequence elements present in the version but deleted now → `ElementInsert` of new elements
  with the old values at the old position (fresh element ids: tombstones are not resurrected);
  elements added since → `ElementDelete`; elements whose registers differ → `SetFields`
  through the element path;
* text differing → `TextDelete` of the characters not in the version and `TextInsert` of the
  version's characters that are missing, anchored by the surviving neighbours, then
  `TextMark` ops for formatting that differs.
+
The diff skips the `comments (0:12)` collection entirely (xref:comments.adoc[Comments],
COLLAB-032): threads are conversation, not state to roll back.
+
Because every op is an ordinary op from the restoring user's replica, it merges with
concurrent edits register by register: the restore wins where its OpId is greater (it is
newest at the moment it is made), and a collaborator's *later* edit wins over it in turn.  The
restore is one undo step.  A restore never references collected tombstones, so it is safe
against garbage collection by construction.

Version naming::
Server rows; two concurrent names are two versions.  A version named offline carries
`through_local_change`; the server resolves it to the seq at which that change landed, which
may be interleaved with others' changes -- the version then means "the state including
everything up to and including my change", which is the closest faithful meaning.

Blame::
`ListNodeHistory` reads the retained log by node id (the server indexes `change_log` by
touched node ids, COLLAB-020).  Losing writes appear too, marked, since the log retains them.

Read-time normalization: none.

=== Offline behavior

The local `changes` table is the history the panel shows offline: remote changes since the
local snapshot plus the outbox, marked *Not yet synced*.  Older history is fetched
on demand when online and cached per session, not persisted.  Naming a version offline
queues a `NameVersion` in a small `pending_calls` table (COLLAB-024) keyed by `version_id`;
it is sent after the outbox drains so `through_local_change` has been sequenced.  Viewing and
restoring work offline for any seq the local log can rebuild (the local snapshot's seq and
later); older rows show *Available when online*.  Restore as Copy needs the network.

=== Server

SRV-011 delivers named versions as bookmarks and restore-as-copy.

As built (SRV-011, `api/.../history`): `NameVersion` records the head for `server_seq` 0, refuses a
seq beyond the head (`HISTORY_UNAVAILABLE`), and resolves `through_local_change` to the
`server_seq` of the replica's logged change whose counters it falls in (not in the hot log:
`HISTORY_UNAVAILABLE`); a retry with the same `version_id` answers the version, the id on another
document is `DOCUMENT_EXISTS`.  `ListVersions` pages newest first by `(created_at, id)`.
`PinVersion(true)` materializes a snapshot at exactly the version's seq (the state rebuilt from the
newest snapshot before it plus the tail, including cold segments) unless one exists there, and sets
`pinned`; unpinning clears the flag and leaves the snapshot.  An unknown `version_id` is
`NOT_FOUND`.  `RestoreAsCopy` is `DocumentService.Fork` at the seq with no changes, defaulting to
the caller's personal space and the source's name.  `ListHistory` and `ListNodeHistory` read the
hot log only, so `retained_from_seq` is its oldest row: sessions are one author and one merged
branch with gaps under 10 minutes, newest first, carrying their changes at five or fewer or when
named by `expand_session`, interleaved with the named versions in the page's seq range (a version
above a session ending at its seq); a page reads at most 5,000 rows (a session longer than that
continues on the next page), and `query` matches labels and author names.

As built (COLLAB-020, `api/.../history`: `ChangeIndex`, `History`; V11):

* *The index.*  Every change is indexed as it is written: `change_node` (one row per node the change
  names; primary key document, node, seq) and `node_name` (one row per name a change gives a node:
  a `CreateNode` with its kind and `CommonProps.name`, empty when unnamed, or a `SetFields` writing
  `CommonProps.name`, empty when cleared).  The ingest statement writes both in the same statement as
  the log row; a branch merge's replay and a copy's extra changes write them in their own
  transaction, and a copy (Fork, Duplicate, branch, Restore as Copy) takes the source's `node_name`
  rows up to the fork point.  The index is not compacted, so it covers cold history.  Changes logged
  before V11 are not indexed (no backfill: the log predates any production data).
* *Timeline across cold segments.*  `ListHistory` reads the hot rows, then the cold segments below
  them, as one log; `retained_from_seq` is the oldest seq either holds.  A cold change's time is its
  own `wall_time_ms` (the client's clock; the hot row's server time is not in the segment), and its
  author and merged branch come from the replica binding (on the document, or on the branch for a
  replayed change).
* *Rows.*  Each `ChangeSummary` names up to 8 touched nodes with their names at the change's seq (the
  newest `node_name` row at or before it; an unnamed node shows its kind's field name, "Path",
  "Master page"), and `attributes` lists the registers written: the field a `SetFields` path names
  under the kind (under `CommonProps`, the common field: "Name", "Transform"), the sequence, text or
  set field of the other field ops, "Deleted" for `SetDeleted` and "Order" for `MoveNode`, at most 32.
  Deviation: the merge table carries no display names, so the names are the proto field names made
  readable (`stroke_width` is "Stroke width"); the client localizes them.
* *Search.*  `query` also matches a change that touched a node whose name, at any time, contains it.
* *Node history.*  `ListNodeHistory` reads the node's seqs from `change_node` (newest first,
  `page_size` at a time) and fetches only those changes, hot or from their segments.
* Not built here: the change retention job with the team window, and retaining pinned versions'
  blobs past it (COLLAB-020's remaining items); losing writes are not marked in node history.

COLLAB-020 adds:
`ListHistory` with server-side session grouping and search, a touched-node index on
`change_log` for `ListNodeHistory`, `attributes` derivation from field paths, offline
`NameVersion` resolution through `through_local_change`, snapshot materialization for named
versions (`pinned`) before their changes age out, the 30-day change retention job with the
team setting, and the rule that blobs referenced by a pinned version are retained.

=== Client

* `WTApp/History`: `HistoryPanel` (SwiftUI list with session rows, expansion, search, multi-
  select for compare), `VersionWindow` (read-only document window over a scratch `WTModel`
  built from a snapshot, with the restore bar), `NameVersionSheet`, the `BlameLine` and
  `NodeHistoryPopover` in the Object panel.
* `WTModel.RestoreCommand` computes the diff-as-change above on a background actor and
  reports the summary counts before committing.
* `WTSync.HistoryCache`: pages of `ListHistory` and `ListNodeHistory` keyed by seq range,
  invalidated by incoming `SequencedChange`s (which are appended locally without a fetch).
* Undo grouping: a restore is one change; naming a version is not undoable (it is a server
  call; delete instead).
