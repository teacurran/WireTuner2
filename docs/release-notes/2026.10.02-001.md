# WireTuner 0.1.0 beta

## Fixed in this build
- **Clicks on panels no longer reach the canvas.** Clicking a tool, right-clicking a panel's title bar or the toolbox, or scrolling over a panel's edge used to act on the canvas underneath as well. That opened the canvas's context menu, panned the document, or left the canvas thinking the mouse button was still held. Panels and docks now keep every click, right-click and scroll to themselves.
- **No more runaway scrolling.** A canvas drag whose mouse-up was lost made the document scroll away at top speed, and it was hard to stop. Such a drag now ends cleanly. Auto-scroll stops when you release the button, press Escape or switch to another window, and moves at most half as fast as before.
- **The canvas has edges.** It now extends around your pages by half their size on each side, at least the width of an A4 sheet left and right and its height above and below. That leaves room to keep artwork beside the pages without losing them:
  - Scrolling, zooming out, the Hand tool, trackpad momentum and Fit All all stay within it.
  - Artwork already outside it, or dragged or pasted past its edge, extends it, so nothing is ever out of reach.
- Everything from 2026.10.01-001: the Layers panel lists, names and arranges every object, and FreeHand files draw as FreeHand did.

## Using it without a server
There is no WireTuner server yet, and the app works on its own:
- Your documents are saved automatically on this Mac and listed in the Library window.
- Export (PDF, SVG, PNG and more) and printing work normally.
- Save Version and the History panel keep versions on this Mac.
- Sharing, comments, branches, restoring versions, publishing links and team libraries need a WireTuner account; they are shown but disabled with that reason.
- There are no automatic updates yet; new builds are published on the GitHub releases page.

## What to try
- Click, right-click and scroll on the toolbox and panels while working; only the panel should respond.
- Scroll and zoom out as far as you can: the pages should always stay within reach.

Please report problems with what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
