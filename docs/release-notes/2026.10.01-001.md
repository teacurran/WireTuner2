# WireTuner 0.1.0 beta

## New in this build
- **The Layers panel lists every object.** Each layer opens with its disclosure triangle to show its objects, frontmost first. Groups, clip groups, blends, charts and text open to their contents too, and Option-click opens everything below.
  - Click a row to select the object, Cmd-click to add or remove, Shift-click for a range. Selecting on the canvas opens the groups above the object and highlights its row.
  - **Name any object or group**: double-click its name in the panel, or choose Rename… from the row's menu. Unnamed objects show their kind ("Path", "Group", the start of their text) in italics.
  - Drag rows to restack objects, move them between layers, or move them into and out of groups. Each drag is one undo step.
  - The eye on an object row hides it on this Mac only, and the padlock locks it.
  - Find objects by name with the field at the top of the panel.
  - SVG export and import keep object names.
- **FreeHand files draw more faithfully:**
  - Named colours come in as swatches.
  - CMYK colours show on screen as they print, not as over-bright cyan.
  - Strokes a FreeHand style turned off stay off.
  - Compound paths nested inside compound paths keep their cut-outs.
  - Closed paths stay closed.
- **Opening Illustrator, PDF and other files** as documents now brings in their named colours as swatches.
- **Strokes on complex outlines**, such as letters with holes, draw correctly where they used to fill the whole shape.
- Everything from 2026.09.30-001: opening Illustrator, PDF, SVG, EPS, DXF and FreeHand files as documents with their layers, the Publish as HTML fixes, Find & Replace, the transform options, typeface editing and the smaller app.

## Using it without a server
There is no WireTuner server yet, and the app works on its own:
- Your documents are saved automatically on this Mac and listed in the Library window.
- Export (PDF, SVG, PNG and more) and printing work normally.
- Save Version and the History panel keep versions on this Mac.
- Sharing, comments, branches, restoring versions, publishing links and team libraries need a WireTuner account; they are shown but disabled with that reason.
- There are no automatic updates yet; new builds are published on the GitHub releases page.

## What to try
- Open one of your FreeHand files and compare its colours and outlines with FreeHand's.
- Expand a layer in the Layers panel, rename a few objects and groups, and drag them between layers.

Please report problems with what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
