# WireTuner 0.1.0 beta

## New in this build
- **Large documents stay fast.** An edit used to rebuild the whole canvas, and in a document of tens of thousands of objects a rename or a restack could take seconds to minutes. Now an edit only redraws what it touched. A restack on a 50,000-object layer went from minutes to a fraction of a second.
- **Layers panel:**
  - **Keyboard:** the arrow keys move through the rows, Right and Left open and close layers and groups, Return renames, and typing the start of a name jumps to it.
  - **Object › Locate Object** (also on the canvas's right-click menu) shows the selected object's row.
  - **Thumbnails:** each row shows a small preview of its object.
  - **Colour drops:** drop a swatch on a row to fill that object, or hold Cmd to colour its stroke.
- **Import:**
  - FreeHand paths keep their even/odd fill, so shapes with overlapping contours keep their holes.
  - Object names from Illustrator files appear in the Layers panel.
  - Illustrator 2020 and later files open with their artboard names, layers and object names, like older files.
  - A deeply nested Illustrator EPS that could crash the app now opens. No file, however deeply nested, can crash an import.
- **Fonts:**
  - Importing an OpenType or TrueType font brings back its anchors, ligatures and glyph classes, and its other features as editable feature text.
  - Generate Fonts has Fix All Warnings, runs in the background with a Stop button, and reminds you of changes not yet synced.
  - Generated fonts now carry their creation date, a proper `.notdef` glyph, and TrueType dropout control.
  - On a glyph tab, anchors and components move with the arrow keys and delete with Delete. Add Glyph has a Kind pop-up and Add and Open.
- Everything from 2026.10.02-001: panel clicks stay on panels, no runaway scrolling, and the canvas has edges.

## Using it without a server
There is no WireTuner server yet, and the app works on its own:
- Your documents are saved automatically on this Mac and listed in the Library window.
- Export (PDF, SVG, PNG and more) and printing work normally.
- Save Version and the History panel keep versions on this Mac.
- Sharing, comments, branches, restoring versions, publishing links and team libraries need a WireTuner account; they are shown but disabled with that reason.
- There are no automatic updates yet; new builds are published on the GitHub releases page.

## What to try
- Open a large imported file, then rename, recolour and restack objects. Each edit should be instant.
- Use the arrow keys in the Layers panel, and drop a swatch on a row.
- Open your FreeHand and Illustrator files again and check fills and object names.

Please report problems with what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
